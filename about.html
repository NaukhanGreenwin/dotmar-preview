    About Dotmar

    Since 1982.

    The Dotmar Difference: a true specialty fitness equipment supplier. Canadian owned.

    40+

    years in business

    From early beginnings.

    In 1982 Dotmar Fitness Equipment, born out of a love for the sport of weightlifting, opened as a supplier and distributor of weightlifting and fitness equipment and supplies. We grew on three priorities: fitness knowledge, equipment quality and customer service, to become one of today’s leaders in commercial fitness equipment across Canada.

    The majority of our business still comes from word of mouth and repeat customers. If we surpass expectations, our customers come back and bring their friends. That has been proven again and again.

    We are not a department store.

    We cater to the individual or commercial facility that demands top quality, not only in equipment but in information and service. From planning and budgeting to layout, installation and after-sales service, we do it all, with our own staff and our own fleet of trucks.
