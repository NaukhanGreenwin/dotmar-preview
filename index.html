    Born from a love of weightlifting.

    Dotmar opened in 1982 as a supplier of weightlifting equipment and grew on three priorities: fitness knowledge, equipment quality and customer service. Today it is the longest-standing specialty fitness equipment supplier in Ontario, and one of the oldest in Canada.

    Most of our business still comes from word of mouth and repeat customers. We are not the biggest, just the best.

    “When helping to serve any one of our over 250 clubs, Dotmar Fitness Equipment Inc. has proven to be both professional and dependable in their approach. I trust them without hesitation.”

    Shawn MacKinnon, Vice President Operations, GoodLife Fitness Clubs
