    Get a full-body workout with Altas Strength Home Gym Smith Machine 3058G. This smith machine features a Pulley System Gym, Squat Rack, Pull Up Bar, Upper Body Strength Training Leg Developer, Light Commercial Fitness Equipment Included Accessories

    • Olympic weight rack - Consists of 6 weight plate pegs built into the frame, organize your weights/barbells on the machine for easy accessibility (400 lbs weight stack included in the back but not include weight plate)!

    Power Rack Perform squats, bench presses, overhead presses, deadlifts, and more. Constructed of heavy duty gauged rolled steel tubing that is powder coated then clear coated for added durability and longevity.


    Functional Trainer The Functional Trainer and cable crossovers allow you to perform over 75 different exercises. Includes 6 functional trainer attachments and 2 x 200lb integrated weight stacks located at the back of the unit for the ultimate strength training workout. Features 16 adjustable height positions to perform shoulder, arms, chest, back, core, and leg exercises.

    Low Row The vertical leg press attachment quickly transforms into a low row station footpad that combines with either cable pulley system to deliver a full-body workout.
