    Details

    More details.

    Smith Machine 3058G The ALTAS STRENGTH 3058G All-In-One Solution


    Power Rack Perform squats, bench presses, overhead presses, deadlifts, and more. The Power Rack base includes commercial-grade J-Hooks and Safeties which can be adjusted to virtually any height position you need. Removable rubber barbell protector sleeves protect your barbell knurling and extend the life of your barbell. Constructed of heavy duty gauged rolled steel tubing that is powder coated then clear coated for added durability and longevity.


    Functional Trainer The Functional Trainer and cable crossovers allow you to perform over 75 different exercises. Includes 6 functional trainer attachments and 2 x 200lb integrated weight stacks located at the back of the unit for the ultimate strength training workout. Features 16 adjustable height positions to perform shoulder, arms, chest, back, core, and leg exercises.


    Vertical Leg Press The ALTAS STRENGTH offers the only Functional Trainer of its kind to include a leg press attachment that you can use either on a bench or when in use on the floor.（Sold separately）


    Pull-up Station Designed Multi-Grip Chin Up and Pull Up station with a massive 800lb weight rating. Knurled grip positions include wide-grip, close grip, underhand, overhand and a variety of other varied movements.


    Dip Station The Multi-Grip Dip Handle attachments provide different width options for you to choose from. Conveniently adjusts vertically on the power rack and accepts weight belts or resistance bands.


    Low Row The vertical leg press attachment quickly transforms into a low row station footpad that combines with either cable pulley system to deliver a full-body workout.


    Rotating Landmine Includes a rotating landmine station that is suited for all types of rotational torso training and allows for a wide range of upper body training when connected to an optional barbell (not included).
