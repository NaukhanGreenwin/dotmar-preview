    Dynamic Iconic 5° Reverse Hyper

    The Iconic 5° Reverse Hyper® is designed for the health & benefit of the lower back. The 5° pad angle caters to all abilities of student athletes, and allows traction of the spine while rotating the sacrum. Develop spinal erector, lumbar, glutes and hamstrings with no compression of the spine, for stronger, more flexible student athletes.
