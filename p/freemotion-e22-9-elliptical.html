    Overview

    Freemotion e22.9 Elliptical

    Deliver an engaging, total-body workout to your members with this ergonomically-enhanced, low-impact cardio solution. Burn more calories and get fit faster with less joint strain and improved balance and mobility.

    • Controls Touchscreen, On-handle Digital Controls
    • Number of Programs Thousands of Global Workouts and Studio Classes with iFIT
    • Preset Programs Limitless iFIT Training Options
    • Google Maps Enabled
    • Console Language(s) English
    • Featured Languages EN, also DE, ES, FR, IT, PT, RU, ZH
    • Additional Options Quick-start Manual Mode
    • TV Tuner US & Canada: NTSC/ATSC, QAM, Pro Idiom, Set-Top Box Ready International: PAL B/G, PAL I, SECAM D/K, SECAM L, DVB-T, DVB-T2, DVB-C
    • Closed Captioning CC1-4, TEXT 1-4
    • Optional Upgrades N/A
    • Recommended Internet Connection Dedicated Ethernet - Cat 6
    • WiFi Minimum Requirements 10 Mbps for one unit, 6 Mbps/unit for 2-3 units, 4 Mbps/unit for 4-6 units, 3 Mbps/unit for 7 or more units

    Features

    Built in.

    • 01

      Global Workouts

      Explore the world without leaving the room with thousands of inspiring training videos beautifully filmed in stunning locations around the globe, including 70+ countries and all 7 continents. With iFIT, your machine’s resistance on your elliptical can even adjust to match the terrain and instructions of your coach.

    • 02

      Low Impact, Total-Body Training

      Give yourself a total-body exercise with the smooth-flowing motions of this redesigned cardio machine. Cross train your way to your fitness goals while treating your body to a low-impact, joint-friendly workout.

    • 03

      Accessible and Engaging Solution

      Participate in the high-intensity studio classes you've been wanting to attend but may not have felt comfortable joining because the equipment accessibility. With a 9.5 in / 24.1 cm step-up-height and ergonomically engineered 20 in / 50.8 cm stride, you have the equipment to thrive.

    • 04

      High-definition Display

      Immerse yourself in visually stunning imagery while working out in exotic locations around the globe, or watch a favorite TV program on this high-definition 22in / 55cm capacitive touchscreen.

    • 05

      On-the-fly Adjustments

      Override or adapt the programming to meet your needs by quickly adjusting the resistance with controls conveniently placed directly on the handlebars.

    • 06

      Know Your Zone

      Get real-time data during your workout with built-in EKG grips, so you know when you're in the ideal heart-healthy zone. Also compatible with ANT+ and Polar®.

    • 07

      Audio-enhanced Experience

      Deeply immerse yourself in your workout with an enhanced audio experience when connecting via Bluetooth or headphone jack.

    • 08

      Advanced Workout Metrics

      Never lose sight of your personal fitness goals with real-time metrics that go beyond traditional feedback, leveraging heart rate, intensity, incline statistics, and more to measure and track your fitness journey.

    • 09

      Power Up

      Charge your devices during your workout with a convenient USB port.
