    Overview

    The ability to push things overhead is an essential function in life that requires more than isolated shoulder strength. It also requires core strength and stability to protect the spine. The GENESIS Multi-Plane Shoulder helps build strength through the torso and joint stabilizers with its cable-based design. The lack of required adjustments and the extensive cable travel lets the user move from supported to standing to adding rotation and beyond, continuously adding variety and challenge to their program.

    Additional Information

    Freedom to Move
    The swivel pulley design combined with the extensive cable travel allows users a wide, fluid range of motion.

    Movement Variety
    Handles move independently for single and alternating arm exercises.

    Walk Up and Work Out
    With no required adjustments, users can simply walk up, grab the handles and start moving.

    Extensive Cable Travel
    Cable length allows for full flexion and extension. Single arm: 76 in / 193 cm, two arms: 38 in / 96.5 cm.

    Padded, Contoured Seat
    For beginning users or those looking to isolate the lower body, the seat features contoured padding for a comfortable fit.

    Enclosed Weight Stack
    The weight stack is enclosed to limit access to moving parts for safety.

    Built to Last
    Constructed with 11- and 7-gauge steel and electrostatically powder-coated, this machine is built to stand up to the rigors of the weight room.

    Kick Plates
    Large, 16-gauge, stainless steel kick plates protect the machine from wear and tear.
