    Overview

    Freemotion i10.9b Incline Trainer

    Experience 'What's Next' with the new Freemotion SMARTSERIES™ powered by iFIT®. The i10.9b Incline Trainer features -3% decline, +30% incline and easy-to-use 1-STEP™ Controls for HIIT training. The console powered by iFIT® goes from simple to interactive when synced with a user's smart device.

    Features

    Built in.

    • 01

      In-Handle Controls for Speed and Incline

      Convenient in-handle controls allow users to increase/decrease speed and incline/decline without moving their hands from the handles.

    • 02

      Crossflow™ Workout Fan

      The strong, adjustable fan is positioned at the top of the console, allowing for a more effective cooling of the user.

    • 03

      Efficient Lift System

      Enjoy consistent performance and intense durability with this lift motor designed to run stronger and longer.

    • 04

      Tapered Roller Belt Drive Guide System

      The machine's narrowed drive dramatically extends the life of the walking deck.

    • 05

      Pulse Sensors

      Get real time data during the workout with ergonomically positioned, built-in EKG grips.

    • 06

      Retractable, Swivel Casters

      These welcome rollers make it easier for the Incline Trainer to be moved around the facility.

    • 07

      Removable Cup Holders

      Made from flexible Santoprene™ (a mix of plastic and rubber), the Incline Trainer's cup holders are more durable and can be removed for easy cleaning.

    • 08

      Efficient Footprint

      The product width of this Incline Trainer is smaller than previous models. This makes it easier for operators to have several Incline Trainers side-by-side.

    • 09

      Personal Entertainment

      Entertainment capabilities make the workout experience even better. Add an optional 15.6 in / 39 cm Personal HDTV Screen.
