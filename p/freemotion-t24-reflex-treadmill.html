    Overview

    Your members' favorite REFLEX™ Treadmill just got upgraded! With many new features to enhance their experience, including a 24 in / 61 cm HD capacitive touchscreen enabled with iFIT, easier to access 1-STEP™ Controls for quick speed and incline changes, and a speed that goes up to 15 mph / 24 km/h. This treadmill still comes with its proprietary cushioning deck that reduces impact, allowing for a longer, stronger run.

    Additional Information

    Best-in-Class EGYM Integration

    EGYM Coach delivers personalized fitness plans that sync directly to your machine, combining intelligent Trainer Prescriptions with iFIT’s rich workout library for a seamless, premium experience far beyond basic 3- or 5-star integrations.

    Stream while you sweat

    We’ve added streaming services you can watch or listen to while working out. Catch up on your favorite shows while catching up on your step count or listen to your favorite playlist for an added boost.

    Your workouts, your way

    Create your own custom workouts with Workout Creator. Set your speed and incline for a specific number of intervals, sets, and cycles. Hit start and your treadmill will automatically take you through your own workout. (Available on other equipment types later this fall)

    Thousands of Coach-led Workouts

    Access an expansive library of on-demand workouts led by expert fitness coaches from around the world. Search or sort by location, trainer, exercise type, workout duration, intensity, and more!

    World-renowned Fitness Coaches

    We've hand-selected 100+ Olympic champions, celebrities, physical therapists, marathoners, and location guides to be your iFIT coaches. With unique training styles and personalities that motivate and inspire, you'll quickly find your new favorite trainer.

    Global Workouts

    Explore the world without leaving the room with thousands of inspiring training videos beautifully filmed in stunning locations around the globe, including 40+ countries and all 7 continents. Your machine's incline will even adjust to match the terrain and instructions of your coach.

    Travel the World

    Transform your workout into an engaging and educational experience.

    Work Out in Breathtaking Locations

    An exciting and immersive fitness experience.

    Participate in Iconic Races

    Challenge yourself by virtually participating in iconic races like the Boston Marathon.

    High-energy Studio Classes

    Experience the hype of a high-intensity studio class alongside other participants and taught by fitness instructors that motivate and inspire you to finish strong. Let your coach guide you through the workout and virtually control your machine.

    Google Maps™

    Run anywhere in the world with Google Maps. Create a route, and iFIT will adjust your treadmill's incline to match the on-screen terrain, allowing you to virtually explore 10 million miles (16 million km) of mapped locations. Revisit your childhood neighborhood or train for an out-of-town race (5K, marathon, etc.) before completing it in person.

    Auto-adjusting Technology

    Each interactive workout allows your coach to remotely adjust the incline or speed of your treadmill to mirror the on-screen content, creating an immersive and engaging training experience.

    Content On-demand, 24/7

    Get access to your favorite fitness coach on your schedule, whenever you choose to workout.

    Progressive Workouts

    Complete a full workout series that continuously challenges with each new workout.

    Ever-expanding Content Library

    With an extensive and ever-growing library of engaging on-screen content, you'll always have a new workout to experience or a unique location to explore.

    A Safer, Healthier Running Experience

    Easier on hips, knees, and ankles, the REFLEX™ proprietary cushioning deck absorbs shock to help run longer and stronger. Providing a 52% greater reduction in tibial shock compared to flat surface running,* the REFLEX Deck is the longevity solution for walking or running.

    Impact-reducing Cushioning

    "REFLEX Cushioning has shown to average a 29% greater reduction in impact force compared to running on competitor's treadmills.*

    *Based on a Freemotion-commissioned university study comparing impact of running on a Freemotion REFLEX™ series treadmill, a Life Fitness 95TS, a Precor TRM 835, and flat surfaces."

    Train with confidence knowing the patented deck tech has also shown to average a 20.5% greater reduction in tibial shock compared to the competition.*

    High-definition Display

    Immerse yourself in visually stunning imagery while working out in exotic locations around the globe, or watch a favorite TV program on this high-definition 24 in / 61 cm capacitive touchscreen.

    HIIT-friendly Cardio

    Great for HIIT sessions or anyone who doesn't want to continually tap the speed+ button, quickly adjust your speed or incline with the convenient 1-STEP™ Controls. If you're looking for a more precise speed or incline, hit two back-to-back. For example, pressing '2' and '5' will adjust the speed to 2.5 mph / km/h.

    Shift Gears Quickly

    Quickly achieve your desired speed by pressing two 1-STEP™ Controls back-to-back – for example, pressing '2' and '5' will adjust the speed to 2.5 mph / km/h – allowing you to quickly reach your ideal speed.

    A Cool Workout

    Stay cool throughout the workout with a conveniently-located 3-speed fan, or select the AutoBreeze™ function to enjoy a variable flow that responds to your pace.

    Advanced Workout Metrics

    Never lose sight of your personal fitness goals with real-time metrics that go beyond traditional feedback, leveraging heart rate, intensity, incline statistics, and more to measure and track your fitness journey.

    Know your Zone

    Get real-time data during your workout with built-in EKG grips, so you know when you're in the ideal heart-healthy zone. Also compatible with ANT+ and Polar®.

    Audio-enhanced Experience

    Deeply immerse yourself in your workout with an enhanced audio experience when connecting via Bluetooth or headphone jack.

    Wireless Charging, Simplified

    Say goodbye to messy cords. Our new wireless charging platform is designed to align your phone perfectly with the charging coil, delivering fast, reliable power every time.
