    Overview

    IronBull Ceramic Clear Dumbbells - Builder

    Made To Order

    Built to Perform. Designed to Impress.

    Our Canadian-made Cerakote Dumbbells bring together durability, performance, and branding in one sleek package. Designed for high-use commercial gyms, these dumbbells give you the premium, stainless steel look—without the premium price tag. With custom colors, logo options, and long-lasting Cerakote protection, they’re more than equipment. They’re a powerful reflection of your brand.

    Options:

    • Logos can be Iron Bull or Custom
    • Handles and Inner and Outer Plate Colors
    • Head Color and Outer Plate Color

    Features

    Built in.

    • 01

      Cerakote Finish = Next-Level Durability

      Cerakote is a ceramic-based coating originally developed for firearms and aerospace gear. Translation? It’s incredibly resistant to corrosion, wear, and fading, even in humid, high-traffic gym environments. These dumbbells won’t chip, flake, or rust the way traditional coatings can. They're built to last through every drop, rep, and client you serve.

    • 02

      Customize Your Look—Your Brand, Your Rules

      Add your logo, choose your color scheme, and make your gym stand out. Whether you’re going for clean and modern or bold and edgy, we help you create a look that fits your space and your identity. Custom dumbbells are a simple way to elevate your environment and reinforce your brand with every lift.

    • 03

      Commercial-Grade Quality, Made in Canada

      These aren’t mass-produced imports. Each pair is manufactured right here in Canada, using precision machining and top-tier materials. They're tested to withstand heavy use in commercial gyms, athletic facilities, and strength training studios. You’re not just buying dumbbells, you’re investing in quality your members can feel.

    • 04

      Stainless Steel Vibe—Without the Price Tag

      Love the sleek, raw look of stainless steel? Our Cerakote Dumbbells offer that same clean aesthetic, but at nearly half the cost. You get the visual appeal and premium experience, while keeping more budget for other upgrades. It’s the smart way to achieve a high-end feel without overspending.

    • 05

      The Right Rack = Longer-Lasting Dumbbells

      Your dumbbells are only as durable as the rack that holds them. That’s why our racks are fully lined and engineered to eliminate metal-on-metal contact, protecting your investment from chips, scratches, and unnecessary wear. Built tough for daily commercial use, they keep your setup clean, safe, and ready for action, no matter how busy your gym gets.
