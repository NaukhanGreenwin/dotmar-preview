    Schiek Carbon Fiber Lifting Belt

    Introducing the CF3006 Schiek Carbon Fiber Lifting Belt: The Epitome of Quality, Comfort, Strength and Support

    The CF3006 Carbon Fiber Lifting Belt is reinforced with an extra layer of Carbon Fiber Marine Vinyl for increased support. This Schiek belt combines strength and durability, offering the toughest support available on the market. With top-of-the-line performance and materials, it's a must-have for serious strength athletes.

    Available in Silver, Black, or White with a black strap, it's a must-have for any serious strength athlete looking for the toughest support on the market. Proudly made in the USA. Comes with a 2-year warranty.

    Built in.

    • 01

      Carbon Fiber Construction

      Crafted from premium carbon fiber materials, the CF3006 offers unrivaled strength and durability while remaining remarkably lightweight. Experience maximum support without compromising on flexibility, allowing you to lift heavier weights with ease and confidence.

      Contoured Design for Perfect Fit

      Engineered with a contoured design, the CF3006 conforms to your body's natural shape, ensuring a snug and comfortable fit every time you lift. The adjustable buckle closure allows for easy customization, providing a personalized fit that enhances stability and reduces the risk of injury.

      Enhanced Stability and Performance

      Whether you're performing squats, deadlifts, or overhead presses, the CF3006 Lifting Belt delivers exceptional stability and core support, allowing you to maintain proper form and technique throughout your lifts. Feel the difference as you push your limits and achieve new personal bests.

    • 04

      Sleek and Stylish Design

      Designed with both form and function in mind, the CF3006 features a sleek and stylish design that sets you apart in the gym. Elevate your workout attire with this timeless yet modern lifting belt, exuding confidence and professionalism with every rep.

    • 05

      Versatile and Reliable

      Suitable for a wide range of lifting activities, including powerlifting, bodybuilding, and functional fitness, the CF3006 is the ultimate choice for athletes of all levels. Whether you're a seasoned lifter or just starting out, this lifting belt provides the support & reliability you need to succeed.
