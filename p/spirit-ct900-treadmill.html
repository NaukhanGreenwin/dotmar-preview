    Overview

    Spirit CT900 Treadmill

    The CT900 Treadmill with robust commercial-grade construction, powerful motor and outstanding warranty allows owners to feel confident they have products that will stand the test of time. It features a premium ultra-bright blue LED display with built-in cooling fan, integrated reading rack with C-Safe port and power port for TV remote and heart rate graph bar. Users can easily select from multiple preset programs that provide a wide variety of popular workouts for the average user while fitness tests, heart rate programs and a custom program are available for more serious users.

    Six polyurethane shock absorbers evenly distributed throughout the unit and a roomy 22” x 62” workout surface ensure a comfortable experience. Oversize pulleys and 12 groove poly-v belt with tensioning idler reduce stress on the system for added reliability. Double-sided .5mm phenolic coated deck, 3” rollers and multi-ply Habasit® belt combine to provide a low maintenance system for thousands of miles. The industrial, 5.0 horsepower AC drive system provides power and longevity. Feel confident in your equipment with the robust and feature-rich CT900 Treadmill.

    Features

    Built in.

    • 01

      Powerful drive

      Industrial 5.0 HP AC drive motor, 12mph top speed, and 15 levels of incline for ultimate workout versatility.

    • 02

      Efficient display

      White multi-window LED conveniently displays 9 pieces of data at a time to keep you informed and motivated, while direct access buttons allow you to quickly adjust your speed and incline during your workout.

    • 03

      Heart rate monitoring

      Wireless heart rate transmitter (chest strap sold separately) and ergonomically correct hand pulse sensors help keep your workouts safe and effective.

    • 04

      Durable deck & belt

      1″ phenolic, double-sided, wax-coated deck and multi-ply Habasit® belt for extensive use, while the spacious 22” x 62” workout area accommodates users of all heights.
