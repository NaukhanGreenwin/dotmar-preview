    Overview

    • Large multi-window LED console with direct speed, incline, and sled resistance buttons
    • Slim console design with an intuitive layout for easy navigation
    • Powerful and durable 4.0 HP AC drive system with speeds up to 15.6 mph (25 kph) and a 20% incline
    • Spacious 22” x 58” (56 x 147 cm) running surface with 60 vulcanized rubber slats for added shock absorption

    Additional Information

    The CTSBS900 Slat Belt Sled Treadmill delivers unlimited training variety in a single, commercial-grade machine. A large multi-window LED console provides clear performance feedback, with direct-access speed, incline, and sled resistance keys and a dedicated mode-select button for fast, intuitive transitions. Packed with built-in programs and fitness tests, it keeps users motivated and challenged workout after workout. Its rugged construction and premium components ensure a smooth, consistent running experience—while giving facility owners confidence in long-term durability.

    With speeds up to 15.6 mph (25 kph) and 20% incline, athletes of all levels can push their limits. For maximum intensity, sled trainer mode adds up to 15 levels of resistance in both push and pull. Integrated contact and wireless heart rate monitoring help users stay in their target zone, a built-in USB charging port keeps devices powered, and over-the-air software updates ensure the machine stays current and performing at its best.

    3-IN-1 TRAINING SYSTEM
    The high-performance slat belt design delivers a smooth, consistent feel with exceptional durability for high-traffic commercial environments.

    QUIET, POWERFUL DRIVE SYSTEM
    The smooth 4.0 HP AC drive motor, 15.6 mph (25 kph) top speed, 20% incline, and 15 resistance levels in Sled-Push mode provide ultimate workout versatility.

    21.5" HIGH-VISIBILITY LED CONSOLE
    Clear performance feedback with direct-access speed, incline, and sled resistance buttons for fast, intuitive control.

    Built-in OTA update capability helps keep the console current, future-ready, and continuously improved over time.

    BUILT-IN PROGRAMS & MILITARY-GRADE FITNESS TESTS

    Includes 10 pre-set programs plus fitness tests including Gerkin, WFI, Army, Navy, Air Force, Marines, Coast Guard, Law Enforcement (PEB), and UK Chester Fireman tests.

    USER-FOCUSED CONVENIENCE FEATURES
    USB charging port, reading rack, cup holder/accessory tray, and transport wheels for easy placement. The 400 lb user capacity and commercial-grade construction deliver long-term confidence for facility owners.
