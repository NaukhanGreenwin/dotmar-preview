    Overview

    Spirit CU900 Upright Bike

    The CU900 Upright Bike features a fully adjustable comfort molded seat that can easily and intuitively be raised or lower to accommodate all users. The integrated adjustable fan provides users with a cool workout experience while an accessory tray provides secure placement of keys, smartphones, etc.

    Commercial-grade pedals, cranks and bearings provide outstanding function and durability. The heavy-gauge high-strength steel with durable powder-coat paint frame means this bike was built to stand the test of time. A heavy duty 30 lb flywheel results in more inertia. Owners can feel confident their users will get a reliable and comfortable workout on the CU900 Upright Bike.

    • 1″ Crank Axle
    • Poly-V Belt
    • zero-Cord Operation
    • Hand Pulse Sensors
    • Generator power system allows for cordless operation
    • Contact and wireless heart rate monitoring capabilities
    • 1” crank axle and heavy duty roller bearings for reliability
    • Easy in and out ratcheting pedals with 2-degree pedal inversion
    • Poly-V belt and spring loaded idler for reduced belt tension

    Features

    Built in.

    • 01

      Efficient display

      Premium, ultra-bright blue LED display with built-in fan, integrated reading rack, heart rate graph bar, and CSAFE port and power port for TV hook-up.

    • 02

      Heart rate monitoring

      Wireless chest strap heart rate transmitter compatible and hand pulse sensors help keep your workouts safe and effective.

    • 03

      Durable frame

      A heavy-gauge high-strength steel frame with durable powder-coat paint means this bike is built to stand the test of time, plus it comes equipped with transport wheels for convenience and ease.

    • 04

      Self-generating power

      Convenient cordless generator power system eliminates the need for plug-ins.

    • 05

      Adjustable seat

      A fast adjustment lever below the seat allows you to adjust your position (up/down) as the large ergonomic padded seat keeps you comfortable.
