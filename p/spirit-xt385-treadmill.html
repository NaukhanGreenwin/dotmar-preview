    Overview

    The XT385 is designed to keep you motivated mile after mile. Our large 22" x 60" running surface and 3.5 HP motor give you all the room and power you need for a comfortable workout. The top speed of 12mph and 15 levels of incline are sure to keep you challenged.
    The XT385 comes with the same options as the XT285, but includes additional features like two user programs, console heart rate and muscle activation profiles, up to 15 levels of incline, aluminum side rails, a larger motor with a wider belt, and frame stability crossbar for a more solid feel. The XT385 is compatible with the Zwift app. This model is very appealing to someone that is a walker, at a transitional stage from walking to running, or a mid-level runner.

    Intuitive Display
    7.5” multi-colour ClearView™ LCD Console Display conveniently displays 9 pieces of data at a time to keep you informed and motivated, while the muscle and heart rate activation profiles provide greater insight into your workout.

    Power & Incline
    Our PowerMax™ drive provides a powerful 3.5 horsepower motor, 12mph top speed (0.1 mph increments), and 15 levels of incline (at 0.5 level increments) for ultimate workout versatility.

    Folding Design
    Easily fold and store your treadmill when not in use with the Lift Assist and Safe Drop folding deck technology.
