    Overview

    The Spirit Fitness XT485ENT Folding Treadmill is the perfect mix of features and function. With a large 10.1” touchscreen display you can easily navigate through the 30+ on-board programs and workout controls. Preloaded apps like Netflix, YouTube, CNN, and more, as well as built-in screen mirroring capabilities and a USB charging port offer endless entertainment or motivation opportunities, so you don’t miss a beat. Built-in Bluetooth FTMS lets you connect to apps like Zwift and Kinomap, connect to your headphones, or an external heart rate monitor. The powerful 4.0 HP motor backed by a lifetime warranty will get you moving up to 12 mph on the generous 22” x 60” running surface. Need extra room? The Spirit Fitness XT485ENT packs all of this into a convenient folding-frame design with built-in transport wheels that makes moving and storage a breeze.
