    Overview

    Challenge, Comfort, and Results: The StairMaster 8 Series FreeClimber Experience

    The StairMaster 8 Series FreeClimber delivers a premium, low-impact climbing experience built to target the glutes. Featuring advanced step design with enhanced cushioning and independent pedal geometry, it ensures a smooth, joint-friendly motion that keeps members engaged and challenged. Two Apex Display options offer personalized fitness tracking and entertainment, elevating the workout experience. Perfect for fitness facilities seeking a durable, innovative solution that provides intense, result-driven workouts, the FreeClimber guarantees a climb members will want to conquer every time.

    Display Options: Choose between Apex 16 inch HD Touch or Apex LED Displays that provide interactivity and personalization, delivering a climbing experience designed to match user preferences.

    Tap to Pair: Quickly sync your Apple or Samsung Galaxy Watch using NFC technology. Real-time tracking and seamless data sharing ensure an enhanced and connected climb every session.

    Customizable Climb Performance: With a 1”–14” step range, 20 resistance levels, and speeds from 26–174 steps per minute, every workout pushes limits.

    Landmark Challenge Program: The Landmark Challenge Program allows users to virtually climb iconic landmarks like the Eiffel Tower, Statue of Liberty, and Taj Mahal, adding an exciting, immersive experience to cardio sessions. Standard console programs are also included for variety.

    Patented Independent Pedal Geometry with Four-Bar Linkage: Designed for comfort and biomechanics, the patented independent pedal geometry with four-bar linkage keeps pedals level, ensuring a natural and ergonomically correct movement for all users.

    Command Center: Wireless charger, USB port, cooling fan, and dual bottle holders. Keep fresh, hydrated, and connected during intense workouts.

    Additional Information

    StairMaster 8 Series FreeClimber – OpenHub™ Technology

    The 8 Series FreeClimber connects to the OpenHub ecosystem, delivering next-level performance with Touchscreen and LED console options. The Touchscreen display features wireless screen mirroring, interactive content, and smart connectivity, while the LED display provides Quick Key adjustments and essential workout metrics. Both include over-the-air updates and optional real-time data insights with Ecofit for peak efficiency.
