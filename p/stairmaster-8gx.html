    Overview

    Stairmaster 8Gx

    StairMaster 8Gx: Elevate Cardio and Strength Training in One Powerful Machine

    The StairMaster 8Gx sets the standard in the stepping category—performance, durability, and design. Built for high-intensity cardio and lower-body strength, it features a bold, modern aesthetic and rugged, sweat-resistant construction that stands up to the toughest workouts while providing smooth, quiet performance. Ergonomic handrails support multiple climbing styles, and the precision-engineered drive train ensures every step is powerful and controlled. This isn’t just another stair climber—it’s the one members recognize, trust, and push themselves on, rep after rep.

    Step into the OpenHub ecosystem with two console options—Touchscreen and LED—built for next-level performance. The Touchscreen display delivers personal device mirroring, interactive entertainment, and seamless connectivity, while the LED display offers Quick Key adjustments and streamlined workout tracking. Both include over-the-air updates and optional real-time data insights with Ecofit to keep your facility ahead.

    Features

    Built in.

    • 01

      Display Options

      Choose from Apex 16 inch HD Touch or Apex LED Display with enhanced interactivity, providing users with an engaging, personalized cardio experience for every climb.

    • 02

      Tap to Pair

      Connect your Apple or Samsung smartwatch instantly with NFC for seamless data sharing. Track your climbs in real time, unlocking accurate metrics and an enhanced cardio experience tailored to your goals.

    • 03

      Landmark Challenge Program

      The Landmark Challenge Program allows users to virtually climb iconic landmarks like the Eiffel Tower, Statue of Liberty, and Taj Mahal, adding an exciting, immersive experience to cardio sessions. Standard console programs are also included for variety.

    • 04

      Handrail Design

      An ergonomically designed handrail system offers support for multiple climbing positions, enhancing comfort and versatility in each workout.

    • 05

      Engaging Content

      The touchscreen display offers onboard HIIT programming, wireless personal device mirroring, and integrated apps like RunTV and PlutoTV—ensuring an engaging and personalized experience for every user, from casual gym-goers to dedicated athletes.

    • 06

      Command Center

      Wireless charger, dual bottle holders, USB port, and cooling fan. Designed for seamless hydration, comfort, and device charging during your toughest climbs.
