    Overview

    Star Trac Upright Bike: Reliable Performance for Every Fitness Facility

    The Star Trac 8 Series Upright Bike is engineered for long-lasting performance in high-traffic fitness facilities. Featuring the ultra-durable Morse Taper bottom bracket, it ensures stability and reliability. Ergonomically designed for a smooth, natural ride, this bike accommodates all fitness levels with ease. Its sleek, modern design and user-friendly interface make it the perfect solution for facilities looking to provide an efficient, high-performance cardio experience. Deliver dependable workouts and superior comfort with the Star Trac 8 Series Upright Bike—the go-to choice for lasting results.

    Display Options: Choose between Apex 16 inch HD Touch or Apex LED Displays to deliver personalized interactivity, enhancing the cycling experience for all users.

    Engaging Content: The touchscreen display offers onboard HIIT programming, wireless personal device mirroring, and integrated apps like RunTV and PlutoTV—ensuring an engaging and personalized experience for every user, from casual gym-goers to dedicated athletes.

    Tap to Pair: Effortlessly pair your Apple or Samsung smartwatch via NFC for real-time workout tracking. Seamless integration delivers accurate metrics and a personalized cardio experience with every ride.

    Easy-Up Seat Adjustment: The easy-up seat adjustment feature allows users to change seat positions effortlessly and efficiently, ensuring maximum comfort and a customizable fit.

    Commercial-Grad Durabilty: Built with the ultra-durable Morse Taper bottom bracket and pedal system, this bike is designed for stability and longevity in high-traffic fitness facilities.

    Command Center: Wireless charger, cooling fan, USB port, and bottle holders. Power your pedaling while keeping devices charged and hydration close at hand.

    Additional Information

    Star Trac 8 Series Upright Bike – OpenHub™ Technology

    Two console options, Apex touch or LED displays, launch the 8 Series Upright Bike into the OpenHub ecosystem, ensuring seamless connectivity and top-tier performance. The Touch display offers personal device mirroring, entertainment streaming, and real-time tracking, while the LED display focuses on Quick Key resistance control and essential performance metrics. Both provide over-the-air updates and optional real-time data insights with Ecofit to keep facilities ahead.
