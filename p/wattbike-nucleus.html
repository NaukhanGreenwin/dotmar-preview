    Overview

    WATTBIKE NUCLEUS AIR TRAINER
    The Wattbike Nucleus features our high-definition Performance Touchscreen, delivering the most advanced performance feedback for a fully connected user experience.

    The standard resistance option of the Wattbike Nucleus has been engineered to better fit the needs of all health and fitness users.

    The high resistance option of the Wattbike Nucleus has been engineered to fit the needs of elite athletes and more powerful riders.

    Additional Information

    Simple Set Up - With only four setup adjustments and easy lock levers.. just plug in and ride. Ideal for the whole household

    Performance Touchscreen - Customize your training, from world-class workouts to building your own sessions. Plus get instant data feedback to keep you on track.

    Resistance - Get precise control over your training with easy air resistance changes, via the lever, to set how much power you require for your workout.

    Pedaling Technique Feedback - Get instant, visual feedback on your left and right leg balance and pedal stroke efficiency to improve your riding technique.

    Choose your Pedals - Toe cages or cleats, you've got the choice, The Nucleus comes with combination pedals so you can ride in trainers or cycling shoes

    Performance Touchscreen

    Your all-in-one coach. Our training ecosystem packed with workouts, tests and a custom workout builder.

    INSTANT FEEDBACK

    Measure and track your progress with data that’s important to you - w/kg, FTP, calories burned, max HR. Plus get visual feedback of your pedalling technique and left and right leg balance.

    CREATE YOUR OWN WORKOUTS

    Custom build your sessions with our interval builder and share them with your fellow Wattbikers.

    ON-DEMAND WORKOUTS

    A variety of sessions to improve your speed, endurance and cycling technique. Plus you can unlock more workouts through the Wattbike Hub app.

    NO MONTHLY SUBSCRIPTION

    Get on-demand access to workouts, plans and tests zero-for.

    ACCURATE DATA With the Wattbike Nucleus you can rely on our data. The Nucleus delivers ±2% accuracy across the max power range of 2000w.
    PERFORMANCE TOUCHSCREEN The touchscreen elevates the cycling experience by delivering incredibly accurate performance feedback. It includes workouts, tests, and Wattbike’s brand new interval builder.
    POLAR VIEW Visualize how you apply force through each pedal stroke and optimize your technique with our patented analysis tool. Training with Polar View helps improve your pedaling efficiency and power output.
    REAL RIDE FEEL Our unique technology replicates the resistance and sensation of riding on the road, delivering the most authentic ride feel available. That means all of the effort you put in indoors easily translates out onto the road.
